# Capitly Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to **denken-serum.2p@icloud.com**, or use Settings → Give Feedback in the app, which adds the app and system version for you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. Capitly has no account and no server, so nobody else can see your entries. See the [privacy policy](privacy.en.md).

### Can I use Capitly on several devices?
Capitly doesn't sync between devices. Each iPhone or iPad keeps its own entries.

### How do I keep my data safe?
Your entries are part of your device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer). If you restore your device from that backup, your entries come back.

### Which features need Apple Intelligence?
Reading receipts, the month and year summaries and the category suggestions for Apple Pay payments. They need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Everything else works on every supported device.

### How do I log Apple Pay payments automatically?
Open Settings → Log Apple Pay Automatically in Capitly and follow the steps. You set up a Shortcuts automation once; after that, payments are logged without you doing anything.

### Why wasn't a payment logged?
- Only payments with the iPhone the automation is set up on, or with an Apple Watch paired to it, are logged. Payments with an iPad or another iPhone aren't.
- Payments in apps or on websites may not trigger the automation.
- Your iPhone needs an internet connection when you pay.
- Payments without an amount, such as Express Transit, can't be logged.

### Siri keeps asking the same question
When Siri has to ask for the category or the amount, it can ask again instead of accepting the answer. This is a known issue in iOS and has been reported to Apple. Name the category right away ("Add a Groceries expense in Capitly") or use the Shortcuts app instead.

### Which currency does Capitly use?
The currency of your device's region.

### How do I delete all my data?
Settings → Delete All Entries removes every entry. Deleting the app removes everything.

### I bought a tip. Does it unlock anything?
No. The tip is a thank-you that supports development. All features are free.

## Privacy

Capitly collects no data. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.en.md).
