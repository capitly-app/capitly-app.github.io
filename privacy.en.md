# Capitly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Capitly is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad. It is built so that your financial data stays with you: there is no account, no server and no tracking. This policy explains what that means in detail.

## In short

- Capitly does not collect any personal data. The developer cannot see your entries, categories or receipts.
- Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
- Capitly contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share data with third parties.
- Apple Intelligence features run entirely on your device.

## Data stored on your device

Your entries (amount, date, description, category, repeat interval), your categories and your settings are stored in Capitly's private storage on your device. They never leave it through Capitly.

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or on a computer, this storage is included in the backup like the data of any other app. Backups are handled by Apple and controlled in your device's settings.

You can delete all entries at any time in Settings → Delete All Entries, or delete everything by removing the app.

## Camera, photos and files

Capitly asks for camera access only to scan receipts. Photos, PDFs and files are only read when you pick them, share them to Capitly or pass them in from a shortcut.

A receipt is read on your device to fill in an entry. Capitly does not keep the image or the file: temporary copies are deleted as soon as the receipt has been read.

## Apple Intelligence

Capitly uses Apple's on-device foundation model to read receipts, write summaries of a month or year and suggest a category for Apple Pay payments. This processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to a server.

## Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

You can add entries with Siri and Shortcuts, and find them in Spotlight. Capitly adds your entries to an on-device search index so they appear in Spotlight; you can remove them from search in the iOS Settings app.

Requests you make to Siri are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Capitly itself receives only the values the request passes in, such as an amount and a category, and stores them as an entry on your device.

## Apple Pay automation

If you set up the optional Shortcuts automation, the Shortcuts app passes a payment's amount, merchant and name to Capitly when you pay with Apple Pay. Capitly saves the amount and the merchant as an entry on your device. The name is only used on device to suggest a category and is not stored.

Capitly does not access your cards, your Wallet or your bank, and it does not use Apple's FinanceKit.

## Tips (in-app purchase)

The optional tip is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details and no personal data from it.

## Contacting support

If you choose Give Feedback in Settings, your email app opens with a message that already contains the app version, build, device model, system version, language and region, to help with troubleshooting. You can read, change or delete this information before you send the email.

When you email us, we receive your email address, your message and the information above. We use it only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering your request).

## Links to other services

## Information Apple provides to developers

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by Apple and by your settings, not by Capitly.
